Honest limits, read this

The overnight price on this page is REAL. It is the Aerodrome price of the Coinbase B20 token for that stock, read from DexScreener, refreshed every 30 seconds when the page is online. When offline it falls back to a labelled snapshot. Overnight trading is NOT thin for these three tokens. In the hour after the US close on 2026-11-16 the top pools did 100 to 400 trades, and 24h volume was $3.6M to $10.7M. The v1 note saying trading was thin was wrong, and it is corrected here. The Chainlink NAV is a reference value, not a trade price. It is price times the token multiplier, it only updates on a 0.5% move or a 24-hour heartbeat, and it can be hours old. At the snapshot time the AAPL NAV was 3 hours 24 minutes old. The gauge uses the DEX trade price, not the NAV. This is not investment advice. Pre-Bell predicts a gap, it does not tell anyone to buy or sell.
